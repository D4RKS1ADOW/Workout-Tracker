Wk 5+

Start at the listed weight for 8 reps. Add one rep per set each week. When every set of an exercise hits 12 reps, don't just add weight — you're on dumbbells at home, so make the rep itself harder:

  • Slow the lowering phase to 3–4 seconds
  • Add a 1-second pause at the bottom of the rep
  • Bump to 4–5 sets instead of adding load
  • Switch to single-arm or single-leg variations
  • Load a backpack with books or water bottles for push-ups and squats
